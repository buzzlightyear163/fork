    b77c10mainquestions
    Before you ask.
    
      
    
        
    Isn’t this just copying?
    No. Copying is taking someone’s name, logo and words. A fork takes the shape of what works: the layout, the mechanic, the live parts. FORK blocks names and tickers that match the parent, never uses the parent’s image, writes every line new, and signs every fork with where it came from.
    
        
    Does it copy the parent’s actual code?
    No. It reads public market data from DexScreener: name, ticker, market cap, links and profile. Then it rebuilds the site with one of FORK’s own engines. The AI rebuild on the roadmap will read the parent’s whole site, under the same rules.
    
        
    Which coins can I fork?
    Any Solana token with a market on DexScreener. Paste its contract address, its pump.fun or DexScreener link, or search by ticker.
    
        
    Do I own my fork?
    Yes. You download one HTML file with your site. Put your contract address and X link in the CONFIG block at the top, then host it anywhere: Vercel, Netlify or GitHub Pages.
    
        
    Will a fork make my coin go up?
    No one can promise that, and FORK doesn’t. It gives you a good site fast. Nothing here is financial advice.
    
        
    Will FORK DM me?
    Never. No presale, no airdrop, no support account. Anyone messaging you as FORK is not us.
